[size=5][b]Description:[/b][/size]
Climb over things. The obstacle is measured the moment you ask, so the climb is exactly as high as the thing you are climbing — a kerb, a fence, a crate, a wall you took at a run. Nothing is hand-placed and nothing snaps to preset heights.

[size=5][b]Features:[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]You climb low fences!
[*]You climb tall walls if you jump at them first!
[*]The height is measured, not guessed — objects nobody thought about still work!
[*]Sprint into something and you vault it on the run!
[*]Low things get stepped over, tall things get hauled — different animations!
[*]10 climb sounds, picked at random!
[*]The camera dips as you go over — and your mouse still works!
[*]No ESP!
[/list]

Works great with [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/newvegas/mods/85459]Enhanced Movement[/url]
[b]Player Physics is required, not optional[/b] — it answers whether you are actually getting anywhere!

[size=5][b]Requirements:[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]xNVSE
[*]JIP LN NVSE
[*]JohnnyGuitar NVSE
[*]Player Physics
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/newvegas/mods/93642]MCM Extender[/url] (optional — for the menu)
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/newvegas/mods/85459]Enhanced Movement[/url] (optional — for the sprint animation)
[/list]
