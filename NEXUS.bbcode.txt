[size=5][b]Description:[/b][/size]
Climb over things.

The obstacle is measured the moment you ask,
so the climb is exactly as high as the thing you are climbing —
a kerb,
a fence,
a crate,
a wall you took at a run.

Nothing is hand-placed and nothing snaps to preset heights.

[size=5][b]Features:[/b][/size]
[list]
You climb low fences!
You climb tall walls if you jump at them first!
The height is measured, not guessed — objects nobody thought about still work!
Sprint into something and you vault it on the run!
Low things get stepped over, tall things get hauled — different animations!
10 climb sounds, picked at random!
The camera dips as you go over — and your mouse still works!
No ESP (ESPLESS)!
[/list]

Works great with [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/newvegas/mods/85459]Enhanced Movement[/url]

[b]Player Physics is required, not optional[/b] — it is what answers whether you are actually getting anywhere, and an older build stops this working entirely!

[/list]
