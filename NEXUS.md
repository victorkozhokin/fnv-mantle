Description:
Climb over things. The obstacle is measured the moment you ask, so the climb is
exactly as high as the thing you are climbing — a kerb, a fence, a crate, a wall
you took at a run. Nothing is hand-placed and nothing snaps to preset heights.

Features:

You climb low fences!
You climb tall walls if you jump at them first!
The height is measured, not guessed — objects nobody thought about still work!
Sprint into something and you vault it on the run!
Low things get stepped over, tall things get hauled — different animations!
10 climb sounds, picked at random!
The camera dips as you go over — and your mouse still works!
No ESP!

Works great with Enhanced Movement﻿
Player Physics is required, not optional — it answers whether you are actually
getting anywhere!

Requirements:

xNVSE
JIP LN NVSE
JohnnyGuitar NVSE
Player Physics
MCM Extender (optional — for the menu)
Enhanced Movement (optional — for the sprint animation)

Installation:

Install with MO2
MCM-RU folder is optional Russian menu text
